01 / MOTIKAM Digital Concert Experience

ESKI ZOT IN PARET

“Breaking the Boundaries of Live Events”

During the COVID-19 pandemic, organizing a concert without a physical audience was a challenge. Conceptualized and managed an online concert featuring more than 5 artists, broadcast live on Facebook to maintain greater control over audience access.

Key Achievements & Responsibilities

  • Delivered a live digital concert reaching over 1,200 live viewers.
  • Managed live-streaming performance and technical challenges.
  • Monitored and engaged audience through real-time comment moderation.
  • Coordinated e-commerce processes and access validation.
Result: Reached 1,200+ viewers in real time Live Broadcast
